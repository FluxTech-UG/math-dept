# MD_0094: the two-stage cascade realisation is exact, bounded and converges fast with a static remainder

clauses (1) and (2) closed, no GAP. Clause (3) is **false as written** for $\omega < 0$ and

## 0. Setup and conventions

- (H0) MD_0090: $c_k > 0$, $\xi_k > 0$, $\sum_k w_k = \sum_k c_k/\xi_k < \infty$; $\alpha > 0$,
  $\mathrm{Pr} > 0$, $\nu = \mathrm{Pr}\,\alpha$. Disc ($R = 1$): $w_k = 4/j_{2,k}^2$, $\xi_k = j_{2,k}^2$.
- (Hu) $u \in L^\infty(0,\infty)$, $\|u\|_\infty$ its essential sup.
- $m_a(s) := a/(s+a)$. For $\operatorname{Re}s \ge 0$, $a > 0$: $|s + a| \ge \operatorname{Re}s + a \ge a$, so
  $|m_a(s)| \le 1$ (L0).
- $T_N := \sum_{k>N} w_k(1/a_k + 1/b_k) = (1 + 1/\mathrm{Pr})\alpha^{-1}\sum_{k>N} c_k/\xi_k^2$.

## 1. Clause (1): the cascade bank has transfer function $K$ (uses H0, Hu)

1. One cascade, frozen rates $a, b > 0$, $\zeta(0) = 0$. The impulse response from $u$ to
   $\zeta_2$ is $h(t) = ab\,(e^{-at} - e^{-bt})/(b - a)$ ($a \ne b$), $h(t) = a^2 t e^{-at}$ ($a = b$).
   $h \ge 0$, $\int_0^\infty h = 1$, and $\int_0^\infty h(t)e^{-st}dt = m_a(s)m_b(s)$ for
   $\operatorname{Re}s > -\min(a,b)$ (direct integration, or Laplace-transform the two first-order
   equations: $\hat\zeta_1 = m_a\hat u$, $\hat\zeta_2 = m_b\hat\zeta_1$). Probe (D) recovers
   $m_am_b(i\omega)$ from a time-domain solve to $10^{-7}$ or better, including $a = b$.
2. The bank. $h_k := w_kh^{(a_k,b_k)} \ge 0$, $\int h_k = w_k$, so by monotone convergence
   $H := \sum_k h_k \in L^1(0,\infty)$, $\|H\|_1 = \sum_k w_k = K(0)$, and $H_N := \sum_{k\le N}h_k \to H$ in
   $L^1$ with $\|H - H_N\|_1 = r_N \to 0$.
3. The sense of the infinite sum. Each cascade is a finite linear ODE with a unique
   zero-state solution $\zeta_{k,2} = h^{(a_k,b_k)} * u$, $|\zeta_{k,2}| \le \|u\|_\infty$ (step 1 or §2).
   So $y(t) := \sum_k w_k\zeta_{k,2}(t)$ converges absolutely for every $t$, $y = H * u$
   (Fubini, $\sum_k\int |h_k||u| < \infty$), and the truncated bank outputs $y_N = H_N * u$ satisfy
   $\sup_t|y - y_N| \le r_N\|u\|_\infty \to 0$: **the truncated cascades converge uniformly in
   time**, for every bounded input.
4. Transfer function. For $\operatorname{Re}s > 0$, $\hat H(s) = \sum_k w_k m_{a_k}(s)m_{b_k}(s)$:
   termwise Laplace transform, justified by dominated convergence with dominant $\sum_k h_k = H \in L^1$.
   This is MD_0090's $K(s)$. The series converges uniformly on $\operatorname{Re}s \ge 0$ by L0 and
   the M-test with $M_k = w_k$, so $K$ is continuous on the closed half-plane and $K(i\omega)$ is
   the frequency response $\int_0^\infty H(t)e^{-i\omega t}dt$. Off the half-plane: for
   $s = re^{i\theta}$, $|\theta| < \pi$, $|s + a| \ge a|\sin\theta|$ when $\cos\theta < 0$, so
   $|m_am_b| \le 1/\sin^2\theta$ uniformly in $k$, and the series converges locally uniformly on
   $\mathbb C\setminus(-\infty,0]$, matching MD_0090's domain. $\square$

## 2. Clause (2): the time-varying bound (uses Hu and $a_k, b_k, w_k \ge 0$; not H0)

A statement about the time-varying system alone; no frozen-rate or MD_0090 structure is used.

1. Solutions. With $a \in L^1_{\rm loc}$, $a \ge 0$, and $u \in L^\infty$, $\zeta_1' = a(u - \zeta_1)$ is a
   linear Carathéodory ODE with locally integrable coefficients; its unique absolutely continuous
   solution with $\zeta_1(0) = 0$ is, with $A(t) := \int_0^t a$,
   $$\zeta_1(t) = \int_0^t a(\tau)e^{-(A(t)-A(\tau))}u(\tau)\,d\tau .$$
2. Convex-combination bound. The kernel is $\ge 0$ and
   $\int_0^t a(\tau)e^{-(A(t)-A(\tau))}d\tau = 1 - e^{-A(t)} \in [0,1]$. Hence
   $|\zeta_1(t)| \le (1 - e^{-A(t)})\,\|u\|_{L^\infty(0,t)} \le \|u\|_\infty$.
3. Second stage, same argument with input $\zeta_1$ (continuous, so its sup is its ess sup):
   $|\zeta_2(t)| \le \sup_{[0,t]}|\zeta_1| \le \|u\|_\infty$. Zero rates are allowed (then $A \equiv 0$
   and the state stays 0).
4. Output. $|y(t)| \le \sum_k w_k(t)|\zeta_{k,2}(t)| \le \big(\sum_k w_k(t)\big)\|u\|_\infty \le
   \big(\sup_t\sum_k w_k(t)\big)\|u\|_\infty$. For infinitely many $k$, $y(t)$ is an absolutely
   convergent series whenever $\sum_k w_k(t) < \infty$; when the sup is $+\infty$ the bound is vacuous
   (and $u = 0$ a.e. gives $\zeta \equiv 0$, $y \equiv 0$). The weights need no regularity in $t$:
   $w_k(t)$ only multiplies $\zeta_{k,2}(t)$ at the same instant. $\square$

The proof gives the stronger pointwise form $|y(t)| \le \sum_k w_k(t)\,\|u\|_{L^\infty(0,t)}$, and it is
sharp: constant $u = 1$ and frozen rates give $y(t) \to \sum_k w_k$ (probe (C)). $\zeta(0) = 0$ is
used in step 1; a nonzero start adds $e^{-A(t)}\zeta_1(0)$, still bounded by the same convex-combination
argument by $\max(|\zeta_1(0)|, \|u\|_\infty)$.

## 3. Clause (3): truncation with static remainder (uses H0)

$K_N(s) = \sum_{k\le N}w_km_{a_k}m_{b_k} + r_N$, $r_N = \sum_{k>N}w_k$ (finite by H0).

1. $K_N(0) = \sum_{k\le N}w_k + r_N = \sum_k w_k = K(0)$, since $m_a(0) = 1$.
2. $K(s) - K_N(s) = \sum_{k>N}w_k\,(m_{a_k}m_{b_k} - 1)$ (both series absolutely convergent).
3. On $s = i\omega$: $m_am_b - 1 = (m_a - 1)m_b + (m_b - 1)$, $|m_b| \le 1$ (L0), and
   $|m_a(i\omega) - 1| = |\omega|/\sqrt{\omega^2 + a^2} \le |\omega|/a$. So
   $$|K(i\omega) - K_N(i\omega)| \le |\omega|\,T_N \quad\text{and also}\quad \le 2r_N .$$
4. **The sign defect.** As written the bound is $\le \omega T_N$. For $\omega < 0$ and any $N$ with
   $r_N > 0$ (all $N$ for the disc, where every $w_k > 0$), $\omega T_N < 0 \le |K - K_N|$: false.
   Moreover $|K - K_N| > 0$ for every $\omega \ne 0$, since
   $\operatorname{Re}(m_am_b - 1) = -\omega^2(\omega^2 + a^2 + ab + b^2)/((a^2+\omega^2)(b^2+\omega^2)) < 0$
   termwise. The repair is $|\omega|$ (conjugate symmetry $K(\overline s) = \overline{K(s)}$ makes the
   $\omega < 0$ case the mirror of $\omega > 0$). Probe (A3) shows the failure at $\omega = -10^{-2}$,
   $N = 1$ for all three Pr.
5. Sharpness at low frequency: $m_am_b - 1 = -i\omega(1/a + 1/b) + O(\omega^2)$, all terms with the
   same sign of imaginary part, so $|K - K_N|/(|\omega|T_N) \to 1$ as $\omega \to 0$ (probe (B):
   $1 - 10^{-9}$ at $\omega = 10^{-2}$, $N = 4, 16$). The bound is informative for
   $|\omega| \ll a_{N+1}, b_{N+1}$; above that the trivial $2r_N$ is smaller.
6. $T_N < \infty$ under H0 whenever $\inf_k\xi_k > 0$ (then $\sum c_k/\xi_k^2 \le \xi_{\min}^{-1}\sum c_k/\xi_k$);
   if $T_N = \infty$ the bound is vacuous. For the disc $\xi_k \ge j_{2,1}^2$.

**Disc, $R = 1$.** $w_k(1/a_k + 1/b_k) = (4/j_{2,k}^2)(1/(\nu j_{2,k}^2) + 1/(\alpha j_{2,k}^2)) =
4(1 + 1/\mathrm{Pr})/(\alpha j_{2,k}^4)$, as the brief says.

7. Rigorous rate. $v(x) = \sqrt x J_2(x)$ solves $v'' + (1 - 15/(4x^2))v = 0$; the coefficient is $< 1$,
   so by Sturm comparison with $\sin$ consecutive positive zeros are more than $\pi$ apart. With
   $j_{2,1} = 5.13562\ldots > 1.6\pi = 5.02655\ldots$ this gives $j_{2,k} > (k + 0.6)\pi$, and
   $\sum_{k>N}(k+0.6)^{-4} \le \int_N^\infty(x+0.6)^{-4}dx = 1/(3(N+0.6)^3)$. Hence for all $N \ge 0$,
   $$T_N \le \frac{4(1 + 1/\mathrm{Pr})}{3\pi^4\,\alpha\,(N + 0.6)^3},\qquad
   |K(i\omega) - K_N(i\omega)| \le \frac{4|\omega|(1 + 1/\mathrm{Pr})}{3\pi^4\alpha(N+0.6)^3}
   \approx 0.01369\,\frac{|\omega|(1+1/\mathrm{Pr})}{\alpha (N+0.6)^3}.$$
8. Constant and exponent. McMahon, $j_{2,k} = (k + 3/4)\pi + O(1/k)$ (confirmed: the brief's
   $(k + 3/4)\pi$ is $(k + \nu/2 - 1/4)\pi$ at $\nu = 2$), gives
   $T_N\,N^3 \to 4(1+1/\mathrm{Pr})/(3\pi^4\alpha)$: exponent 3, constant $4/(3\pi^4)$, so the clause's
   $O(\omega(1+1/\mathrm{Pr})/(\alpha N^3))$ holds (with $|\omega|$) and is the true order. Probe: the
   ratio $T_NN^3/(4(1+1/\mathrm{Pr})/(3\pi^4\alpha))$ is 0.798, 0.944, 0.985, 0.996 at $N = 16, 64, 256, 1024$
   (increasing to 1, like $(N/(N+3/4))^3$), and the bound in step 7 holds for $N = 0..1024$.
   For scale, $r_N \sim 4/(\pi^2N)$, so the static remainder carries a weight of order $1/N$ while
   the frequency error is of order $|\omega|/(\alpha N^3)$.

## Formalization blockers

- MD_0090 itself rests on MD_0065's spectral representation of the Dirichlet resolvent; the triage
  record waives S4 on that ground. Clause (1)'s identification with $K$ inherits it.
- Clause (3) abstractly (summable $w$, positive $a, b$, $\omega \in \mathbb R$) is reachable in Mathlib:
  `tsum` over $\mathbb N$, complex norm estimates, `Summable.of_norm_bounded`. The disc rate is not:
  Mathlib has no Bessel functions or zeros, and Sturm comparison is not in Mathlib.
- Clause (2) needs Carathéodory solutions of linear ODEs with $L^1_{\rm loc}$ coefficients, absent
  from Mathlib; a formal version would take the integral formula of §2.1 as the definition of the
  solution and prove the bound from it (interval integrals, `MeasureTheory.integral_mono`).
- The Laplace-transform identification (§1.4) needs the Laplace transform of an $L^1$ kernel and
  termwise transform under dominated convergence: Mathlib has the Fourier and Mellin transforms
  but no Laplace-transform API for this.
