## Statement

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\text{Let } d \geq 1, \ \Omega \subset \mathbb{R}^d \text{ a bounded Lipschitz domain, } \omega > 0. \text{ For } D > 0 \text{ let } h_D \text{ solve } \\
&i\omega\,h_D = D\,\Delta h_D \text{ on } \Omega \text{ with } h_D - 1 \in H^1_0(\Omega), \text{ and set } F(D) := \langle h_D\rangle. \text{ Fix } \alpha > 0, \text{ put } \\
&\nu = \mathrm{Pr}\,\alpha, \ f_v = F(\nu), \ f_\kappa = F(\alpha), \text{ and } g_{v\kappa} := \frac{(1-f_\kappa) - \mathrm{Pr}(1-f_v)}{1-\mathrm{Pr}} \text{ for } \mathrm{Pr}\neq 1. \\[4pt]
&\text{Then } F \text{ is holomorphic on } \{\operatorname{Re} D > 0\}, \ \operatorname{Re}\big(1 - F(D)\big) > 0 \text{ for } D > 0, \text{ and } g_{v\kappa} \\
&\text{extends holomorphically across } \mathrm{Pr} = 1 \text{ with} \\[4pt]
&\qquad g_{v\kappa}\big|_{\mathrm{Pr}=1} \;=\; 1 - F(\alpha) \;-\; \alpha F'(\alpha)
\;=\; 1 - F(\alpha) - \left[\frac{dF}{d\log D}\right]_{D=\alpha}, \qquad F' = \frac{dF}{dD}, \\[4pt]
&\text{a finite value for every } \omega > 0. \text{ In particular, with } z := L\sqrt{i\omega/\alpha}: \\
&\qquad \text{parallel plates, } d = 1, \ \Omega = (-y_0, y_0), \ L = y_0, \ F = \tfrac{\tanh z}{z}:
\qquad g_{v\kappa}\big|_{\mathrm{Pr}=1} = 1 - F - \tfrac{1}{2}\big(F - \operatorname{sech}^2 z\big); \\
&\qquad \text{the disc, } d = 2, \ \Omega = \{|x| < R\}, \ L = R, \ F = \tfrac{2 I_1(z)}{z I_0(z)}:
\qquad g_{v\kappa}\big|_{\mathrm{Pr}=1} = 2(1 - F) - \tfrac{1}{4}z^2 F^2 .
\end{aligned}
$$

This revises MD_0012, whose corresponding clause carries $+\,\alpha F'(\alpha)$ and is refuted by
`counterexamples/MD_0012_sign_of_derivative_term.py`. The bracket clause of MD_0012,
$\lim_{\mathrm{Pr}\to1}(f_\kappa - f_v)/((1-f_v)(1-\mathrm{Pr})) = \alpha F'(\alpha)/(1-F(\alpha))$,
is correct and is restated here only through the identity
$g_{v\kappa} = (1-f_v)\,(1 - \text{bracket})$.

## Hypotheses

- **H1.** One and the same bounded Lipschitz domain $\Omega \subset \mathbb{R}^d$, $d \geq 1$, carries
  the channel problem at every diffusivity, so $f_v$ and $f_\kappa$ are one function $F$ evaluated
  twice. Taking $d \geq 1$ rather than $d = 2$ is what admits parallel plates as the bounded interval
  $(-y_0, y_0)$; nothing in the statement or its proof uses the dimension.
- **H2.** $\omega > 0$ and $\alpha > 0$. This makes $i\omega - D\Delta$ invertible on $H^1_0(\Omega)$,
  so $h_D$ is unique; it gives $\operatorname{Re}(1 - F(D)) > 0$, hence $F(D) \neq 1$ and the
  denominator of the bracket is bounded away from zero; and it puts $\alpha$ in the interior of the
  region where $F$ is holomorphic.
- **H3.** $\mathrm{Pr} \to 1$ at fixed $\Omega$, $\omega$ and $\alpha$, with $\nu = \mathrm{Pr}\,\alpha$,
  so the limit is in one variable and not a joint limit in frequency and diffusivity.

## Current notes

Successor of MD_0012 with the corrected sign and the setting widened to $\Omega \subset \mathbb{R}^d$, $d \ge 1$; proven informally in `proofs/MD_0016.md`, which reuses the holomorphy, non-degeneracy and limit arguments of `proofs/MD_0012.md` and the modal form (16) of `proofs/MD_0011.md`. Two adversarial reviews by contexts that wrote none of it confirm the value $1 - F(\alpha) - \alpha F'(\alpha)$: finite-difference solves plus a route through the modal double pole on the interval, disc, square, cube and a re-entrant L-shape (2026-09-16), and a later-day cold check (2026-10-08) by symbolic series of the bracket at $\mathrm{Pr} = 1 + \varepsilon$, both closed forms reduced symbolically, difference quotients agreeing to 3e-11 on a rectangle, a 3-ball, plates, disc and an annulus, and a finite-difference solve on a right triangle with a Cauchy-Riemann check of half-plane holomorphy at complex $D$. Route A of the proof (l'Hopital at $\mathrm{Pr} = 1$) carries the limit clause alone. Two wording gaps stand, neither a false step: the Statement declares $F$ only for $D > 0$ and then calls it holomorphic on $\{\operatorname{Re} D > 0\}$, which an S4 formalization must fix by stating $F$ on the half plane (the coercive form in the sector gives solvability there); and the inherited text of `proofs/MD_0012.md` says $w_n > 0$ where $w_n \ge 0$ holds, strict positivity of $\operatorname{Re}(1 - F)$ following from $\sum_n w_n = 1$.
