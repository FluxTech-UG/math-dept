## Statement

With $p_j, r_j \in C^1([0,T])$, $p_j \ge 0$, $r_j > 0$: (a) the output-weighted
realisation $\dot z_j = -p_j z_j + u$, $y = \sum_j r_j z_j$ with storage $\tfrac12\sum_j r_j z_j^2$ obeys
$u y = \frac{d}{dt}\tfrac12\sum_j r_j z_j^2 + \sum_j\bigl(p_j r_j - \tfrac12\dot r_j\bigr)z_j^2$, and it is
passive for every input if and only if $\dot r_j \le 2p_j r_j$ for every $j$ and $t$; (b) the
input-weighted realisation $\dot z_j = -p_j z_j + r_j u$, $y = \sum_j z_j$ is passive for every input
if and only if $\dot r_j \ge -2p_j r_j$, with storage $\tfrac12\sum_j z_j^2/r_j$. The energy created
over $[0,T]$ beyond the storage is at most
$\int_0^T\sum_j \max(0, \tfrac12\dot r_j - p_j r_j)\,z_j^2\,dt$ for (a) and
$\int_0^T\sum_j \max(0, -\tfrac12\dot r_j/r_j^2 - p_j/r_j)\,z_j^2\,dt$ for (b).

## Hypotheses

$C^1$ coefficients, $p_j \ge 0$, $r_j > 0$. \"Passive for every input\" means: for every initial state $z(0)$ and every $u \in L^2(0,T)$, $\int_0^T u y\,dt \ge -V(z(0))$ with $V$ the stated storage; the iff says this fails for some initial state and input exactly when the rate condition fails at some $j$ and $t$. \"Energy created beyond the storage\" is $\max\bigl(0, -\int_0^T u y\,dt - V(z(0)) + V(z(T))\bigr)$.

## Current notes

Refuted (exact): the 'only if' halves of both iffs fail for $n = 2$. `counterexamples/MD_0071_output_weighted_two_state.py` ($p = 0$, $r = (1+t, 4-2t)$, $T = 1/2$; certificate $S = V - t(z_1 - z_2)^2$ with $uy - \dot S = (z_1 - 2z_2)^2/2$) and `counterexamples/MD_0071_input_weighted_two_state.py` ($p = 0$, $r = (1-t, 1+t)$; $\dot y = 2u$) are passive although the per-state rate condition fails. The identities, the sufficiency directions and the created-energy bounds are true; MD_0086 carries them, with necessity for $n = 1$. An adversarial review by a context that wrote neither witness accepted both on 2026-10-18 (`triage/MD_0071.md`).
