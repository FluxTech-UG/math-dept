## Statement

(i) If $G(x) = l^{\top}x$ for some $l \in \mathbb{R}^d$, then $l^{\top}(\Phi(x) - x) = 0$ for every $x \in U$. (ii) If $G \in C^2(U)$ and $B := \sup_{y \in U}\|\nabla^2 G(y)\|_2 < \infty$, then $|\nabla G(x)^{\top}(\Phi(x) - x)| \le \tfrac12 B\,\|\Phi(x) - x\|_2^2$ for every $x \in U$.

## Hypotheses

- $U \subseteq \mathbb{R}^d$ open and convex, $\Phi : U \to U$ any map.
- $G : U \to \mathbb{R}$ with $G(\Phi(x)) = G(x)$ for every $x \in U$.
