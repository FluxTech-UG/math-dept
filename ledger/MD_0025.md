## Statement

Assume f in C^{p+1} and g : R^d x Theta -> [g_min, g_max], 0 < g_min, g in C^{p+1}. Consider in the independent variable s: dy/ds = g(y, theta) f(y, theta), dt/ds = g(y, theta), (y, t)(0) = (y0(theta), 0). Integrate with the explicit RK method (order p) at fixed step Delta s, producing (y_n, t_n) at s_n = n Delta s, until the first N with t_N >= T. The method has a continuous extension u_n(sigma), sigma in [0, 1], polynomial in sigma, u_n(0) = (y_n, t_n), u_n(1) = (y_{n+1}, t_{n+1}), of uniform order >= p, and C^1-glued: for every n, (d/dsigma) u_n(0) = Delta s F(y_n, t_n) and (d/dsigma) u_n(1) = Delta s F(y_{n+1}, t_{n+1}), where F(y, t) = (g f, g)(y, theta) is the right-hand side of the s-system (as for a Hermite or an FSAL extension). Let s* in [s_{N-1}, s_N] be the unique point at which the t-component of the continuous extension equals T, and yhat(T; theta) the y-component there. Claim: theta -> yhat(T; theta) is C^1 on all of Theta.

## Hypotheses

- **H1.** Integers d, m, p >= 1. Theta is an open subset of R^m. f : R^d x Theta -> R^d is C^{p+1}; y0 : Theta -> R^d is C^1. An explicit Runge-Kutta method is given by a Butcher tableau (A, b, c) with A strictly lower triangular, of classical order p, with coefficients independent of theta; Psi_h(y, theta) denotes one step of size h. A grid is 0 = t_0 < t_1 < ... < t_N = T with steps h_n = t_{n+1} - t_n and H = max_n h_n. y_N(theta; h) = Psi_{h_{N-1}} o ... o Psi_{h_0} (y0(theta), theta). y(t; theta) is the exact solution of y' = f(y, theta), y(0) = y0(theta), assumed to exist on [0, T]. S(t; theta) solves S' = f_y(y(t; theta), theta) S + f_theta(y(t; theta), theta), S(0) = d_theta y0(theta). Norms on R^d and on d x m matrices are fixed (any choice; constants depend on it). For theta in Theta and delta > 0, K_delta is the closed delta-neighbourhood of the trajectory {y(t; theta) : 0 <= t <= T}.
- **H2.** g as in the Statement, bounded away from zero; the continuous extension has uniform order at least p and is C^1-glued at the nodes.

## Why it was raised

The repaired statement after the refutation of MD_0022 (MDR:2026-10-16-adaptive-grid-gradient line), for the same application in 1DSim3.

## Current notes

Refuted on 2026-10-17 by the exact witness listed under evidence: Euler with a cubic Hermite extension at Delta s = 1, admissible because the Statement places no smallness on Delta s, has a t-component that is monotone with a stationary inflection where s* sits, so s*(theta) is a cube root and yhat is not differentiable there. The successor is MD_0029 (refuted), and its successor MD_0031, which adds the step bound and proves the crossing transversal. Revises MD_0022, whose claim (a) fails at a node crossing for a non-Hermite extension (Heun witness).
