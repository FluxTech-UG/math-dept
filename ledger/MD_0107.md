## Statement

**Setting.** $V$ is a finite-dimensional vector space over a field $F$, $M : V \to V$ is linear with minimal polynomial $\mu_M$, $c_0 \in V$, and $R(c_0) = \{M^k c_0 : k \ge 0\}$. The Krylov subspace is $\mathcal K(M, c_0) = \operatorname{span}\{M^k c_0 : k \ge 0\}$.

**Statement.** (i) $R(c_0) \subseteq \mathcal K(M, c_0)$. (ii) $\dim \mathcal K(M, c_0) \le \deg \mu_M$. (iii) Every linear functional $\ell \in V^*$ with $\ell \circ M = \ell$ satisfies $\ell(v) = \ell(c_0)$ for every $v \in R(c_0)$. (iv) If $\ell_1, \dots, \ell_r \in V^*$ are linearly independent and each satisfies $\ell_i \circ M = \ell_i$, then $R(c_0) \subseteq A = \{v \in V : \ell_i(v) = \ell_i(c_0),\ i = 1, \dots, r\}$, and $A$ is an affine subspace whose direction $\bigcap_i \ker \ell_i$ has dimension $\dim V - r$ (codimension $r$).

## Hypotheses

- **H1.** $V$ finite-dimensional over a field.
- **H2.** $M$ linear; a single map (the request's case $|U| = 1$).
