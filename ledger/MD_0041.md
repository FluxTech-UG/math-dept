## Statement

For every $g \in \mathbb{C}^N$, $\sum_{j=0}^{N-1} (-1)^j (Dg)_j = 0$.

## Hypotheses

- **(D) Fourier derivative.** $N \ge 2$ is even and $L > 0$. For $n \in \{0, \dots, N-1\}$ put $\bar n = n$ if $n < N/2$ and $\bar n = n - N$ if $n > N/2$, and the symbol $s_n = 2\pi i \bar n / L$ for $n \ne N/2$, $s_{N/2} = 0$. With $\omega = e^{2\pi i/N}$, $D \in \mathbb{C}^{N\times N}$ is $D_{jl} = \frac1N \sum_{n=0}^{N-1} s_n\, \omega^{(j-l)n}$ (indices $j, l \in \{0,\dots,N-1\}$), i.e. $D = F^{-1}\operatorname{diag}(s)F$ with $(Fg)_n = \sum_j g_j \omega^{-jn}$. Then $D$ has real entries and $D^{\top} = -D$ (consequences of the definition, not extra hypotheses), and $K := -D^2 = D^{\top}D$.
