## Statement

**Setting.** $C$ is a finite set of configurations, $|C| = N \ge 1$, and $c_0 \in C$. $M : C \to C$ is a self-map (the case $|U| = 1$ of the request's setting, one input letter), and the reachable set is $R(c_0) = \bigcup_{k \ge 0} R_k(c_0)$ with $R_k(c_0) = \{M^k c_0\}$.

**Statement.** (i) $R(c_0) = \{M^k c_0 : k \ge 0\}$; (ii) $|R(c_0)| \le N$; (iii) there are $\rho \ge 0$ and $p \ge 1$ with $\rho + p \le N$ such that $M^{k+p} c_0 = M^k c_0$ for every $k \ge \rho$ (the sequence $(M^k c_0)_k$ is eventually periodic; $\rho$ and $p$ are some preperiod and period, not necessarily the minimal ones).

## Hypotheses

- **H1.** $C$ is finite and nonempty ($N \ge 1$).
- **H2.** $M$ is any self-map of $C$; no injectivity or other structure is assumed.
