## Statement

Every eigenvalue of $-\big(\alpha K + \operatorname{diag}(u)\,D + \operatorname{diag}(h)\big)$ has negative real part.

## Hypotheses

- **(D) Fourier derivative.** $N \ge 2$ is even and $L > 0$. For $n \in \{0, \dots, N-1\}$ put $\bar n = n$ if $n < N/2$ and $\bar n = n - N$ if $n > N/2$, and the symbol $s_n = 2\pi i \bar n / L$ for $n \ne N/2$, $s_{N/2} = 0$. With $\omega = e^{2\pi i/N}$, $D \in \mathbb{C}^{N\times N}$ is $D_{jl} = \frac1N \sum_{n=0}^{N-1} s_n\, \omega^{(j-l)n}$ (indices $j, l \in \{0,\dots,N-1\}$), i.e. $D = F^{-1}\operatorname{diag}(s)F$ with $(Fg)_n = \sum_j g_j \omega^{-jn}$. Then $D$ has real entries and $D^{\top} = -D$ (consequences of the definition, not extra hypotheses), and $K := -D^2 = D^{\top}D$.
- $N \ge 4$; $u \in \mathbb{R}^N$ arbitrary; $\alpha > 0$; $h \in \mathbb{R}^N$ with $h_j \ge 0$ and two indices $j, k$, $j - k$ odd, $h_j, h_k > 0$.

## In plain words

Spectral diffusion plus absorption at an even and an odd grid point is strictly dissipative (MD_0046, the same operator without advection). This entry claimed that adding advection by a velocity that varies from grid point to point keeps every mode decaying. It is false. On four grid points with weak diffusion (strength 1/100), absorption at two points adjacent on the periodic grid, and a velocity that alternates -3, +3, -3, +3 from one point to the next, the operator has an eigenvalue with positive real part, about 2.57, so one mode grows. A velocity that flips sign at the grid scale defeats both diffusion and absorption. The witness is exact: the characteristic polynomial is factored with rational coefficients and the growing root is decided exactly.

For the consumer, which needs the discretised diffusion-advection operator with partial absorption to have no growing mode, this means diffusion and absorption alone cannot carry that guarantee once the velocity varies in space; a condition on how fast the velocity varies is needed. MD_0064 restates the claim with such a smallness condition (on a commutator), and the known weak instability of Fourier collocation for variable-coefficient advection (Goodman, Hou and Tadmor, 1994) is the published background.

## Current notes

Refuted (counterexamples/MD_0049_alternating_velocity.py, exact): N = 4, L = 2 pi, alpha = 1/100, u = (-3, 3, -3, 3), h = (1, 0, 0, 1); the characteristic polynomial of -(alpha K + diag(u) D + diag(h)) is (200 l^2 - 398 l - 299)(200 l^2 + 802 l + 301)/40000, with the positive root 199/200 + sqrt(99401)/200. A grid-scale alternating velocity defeats diffusion and absorption. Revised by MD_0064 (commutator smallness condition). Context: Goodman, Hou and Tadmor (1994) prove weak L2 instability of Fourier collocation for variable-coefficient advection in conservation form.
