## Statement

Under H1 to H6 with H0' and $h_n \ge 0$ for every $n$, for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and every $\theta \in [0, 1]$,

$$ \big|\ell(u_n(\theta)) - \ell(y_0)\big| \le \delta \Big( \sum_{m < n} h_m \sum_{i=1}^s |b_i| \; + \; h_n \sum_{i=1}^s |\beta_i(\theta)| \Big). $$

With $\beta_i(1) = b_i$ and $B = \sup_{\theta \in [0,1]} \sum_i |\beta_i(\theta)| < \infty$, the right side is at most $\delta \, (t_{n+1} - t_0) \, B$: the elapsed time runs to the end of the step that contains the read point, not to the read point itself.

## Hypotheses

- **H0' (approximate first integral).** A real $\delta \ge 0$ with $|\ell(f(y))| \le \delta$ for every $y \in V$.
- **H1 (field and invariant).** $V$ is a real vector space (the request's $\mathbb{R}^d$ is the case $V = \mathbb{R}^d$), $\ell : V \to \mathbb{R}$ is linear, and $f : V \to V$ is any map. No regularity of $f$ is assumed.
- **H2 (coefficients).** An integer $s \ge 1$, real coefficients $a_{ij}$ and $b_i$ for $i, j \in \{1, \ldots, s\}$ (explicit or implicit: no triangularity is assumed), and dense-output weights $\beta_i : [0, 1] \to \mathbb{R}$, arbitrary functions (polynomials in the application).
- **H3 (accepted steps).** Sequences indexed by $n \in \mathbb{N} = \{0, 1, 2, \ldots\}$: real step sizes $h_n$ (positive in the application; the sign is not used), start points $y_n \in V$ with $y_0$ given, and stage derivatives $k_{n,1}, \ldots, k_{n,s} \in V$. The sequence lists accepted steps only: a rejected trial computes stages and changes no $y_n$, so it does not appear. The end point of step $n$ is $\tilde y_{n+1} = y_n + h_n \sum_{i=1}^s b_i k_{n,i}$.
- **H4 (stages, with FSAL).** For every $n$ and $i$, either $k_{n,i} = f\big(y_n + h_n \sum_{j=1}^s a_{ij} k_{n,j}\big)$ (the stage equation, with $k_{n,\cdot}$ any solution of it), or $n \ge 1$, $i = 1$ and $k_{n,1} = f(z_n)$ with $z_n \in \{\tilde y_n, y_n\}$ (first-same-as-last reuse of the previous step's last derivative, evaluated before or after the post-step map).
- **H5 (post-step map).** Maps $P_n : V \to V$ with $\ell(P_n(v)) = \ell(v)$ for all $v$, and $y_{n+1} = P_n(\tilde y_{n+1})$ for every $n$.
- **H6 (dense output).** On step $n$, $u_n(\theta) = y_n + h_n \sum_{i=1}^s \beta_i(\theta) k_{n,i}$ for $\theta \in [0, 1]$. With $t_0$ given and $t_{n+1} = t_n + h_n$, the piecewise output is $u(t) = u_n((t - t_n)/h_n)$ for $t \in [t_n, t_{n+1}]$ (both pieces apply at a node).

## In plain words

If the right-hand side changes a linear quantity by at most delta at every state, then along any Runge-Kutta run, with any step sizes and any post-step correction that preserves the quantity, the quantity drifts by at most delta times a sum over steps. Each completed step contributes its length times the sum of the absolute values of the method's weights, and the step holding the read point contributes its full length times the sum of the absolute interpolation weights at that point. In elapsed-time form: when the interpolation weights end at the method's weights and B bounds their summed absolute values over the step, the drift is at most delta times B times the time from the start to the end of the step that contains the read point.

This corrects MD_0034, which counted time only up to the read point and fails. The counterexample to MD_0034 meets this bound with equality, so it cannot be tightened in general. For the application that raised it, a measured per-evaluation residual of the right-hand side becomes a guaranteed bound on the drift of a linear ledger over any window, counted to the end of the last step the window touches.

## Why it was raised

The corrected successor of MD_0034 for the same application: a bound on the windowed ledger residual from a measured per-evaluation residual of the right-hand side.
