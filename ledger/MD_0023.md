## Statement

Assume f : R x R^d x Theta -> R^d, C^{p+1}, T-periodic in its first argument t. P(y0; theta) = y(T; y0, theta), the exact period map. y*(theta) is a fixed point of P whose monodromy M = d_{y0} P(y*; theta) has 1 not in spec M. For a grid of [0, T] not depending on y0 or theta, P_h(y0; theta) is the discrete period map of the explicit RK method of order p. Claims: there are H0 > 0, a neighbourhood V of y*, and C1 (H0, V and C1 may depend on everything, M included), and a constant C2 depending only on bounds of the derivatives of f of order up to p+1 on a neighbourhood of the periodic orbit, on T and on the tableau (and not otherwise on M), such that for H <= H0: P_h has a unique fixed point y*_h in V; ||y*_h - y*|| <= C1 H^p; and d_theta y*_h = (I - d_{y0} P_h)^{-1} d_theta P_h satisfies ||d_theta y*_h - d_theta y*|| <= C2 H^p / dist(1, spec M).

## Hypotheses

- **H1.** Integers d, m, p >= 1. Theta is an open subset of R^m. f : R^d x Theta -> R^d is C^{p+1}; y0 : Theta -> R^d is C^1. An explicit Runge-Kutta method is given by a Butcher tableau (A, b, c) with A strictly lower triangular, of classical order p, with coefficients independent of theta; Psi_h(y, theta) denotes one step of size h. A grid is 0 = t_0 < t_1 < ... < t_N = T with steps h_n = t_{n+1} - t_n and H = max_n h_n. y_N(theta; h) = Psi_{h_{N-1}} o ... o Psi_{h_0} (y0(theta), theta). y(t; theta) is the exact solution of y' = f(y, theta), y(0) = y0(theta), assumed to exist on [0, T]. S(t; theta) solves S' = f_y(y(t; theta), theta) S + f_theta(y(t; theta), theta), S(0) = d_theta y0(theta). Norms on R^d and on d x m matrices are fixed (any choice; constants depend on it). For theta in Theta and delta > 0, K_delta is the closed delta-neighbourhood of the trajectory {y(t; theta) : 0 <= t <= T}. Here f also depends on t and is T-periodic in t.
- **H2.** 1 is not an eigenvalue of the monodromy M.

## Current notes

Refuted on 2026-10-17 by the exact witness listed under evidence; the repaired statement is MD_0026, which revises this one. Statement made explicit at triage on 2026-10-17. The factor 1/dist(1, spec M) is informative only when C2 is uniform in M, so C2 is stipulated uniform; H0, V and C1 may depend on M. The uniform form is the one the application relies on, to name the conditioning near marginal stability, and it is the point under S1 (non-normal M, and a squared resolvent in the implicit-function sensitivity).
