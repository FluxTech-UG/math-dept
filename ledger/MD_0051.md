## Statement

$\sigma_{\min}(\theta I - M) \le \varepsilon$, and $E := -(Mu - \theta u)u^{*}$ satisfies $\|E\|_2 \le \varepsilon$ and $(M + E)u = \theta u$.

## Hypotheses

- $d \ge 1$, $M \in \mathbb{C}^{d\times d}$, $\theta \in \mathbb{C}$, $u \in \mathbb{C}^d$ with $\|u\|_2 = 1$, $\varepsilon \ge 0$ with $\|Mu - \theta u\|_2 \le \varepsilon$. $\sigma_{\min}$ is the smallest singular value.

## Why it was raised

Raised by 1DSim4 through C1 of MDR:2026-10-17-nonnormal-residual-certificate.

## Current notes

Proved in Lean (MathDeptPrivate/Results/MD_0051.lean): (a) as a unit vector v with ||(theta I - M) v|| <= eps and as the infimum over unit vectors (sigma_min of a square matrix by its variational definition), (b) with the l2 operator norm, E = -(r u*) via Matrix.vecMulVec. d >= 1 is unused (implied by the unit vector).
