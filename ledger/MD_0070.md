## Statement

Let $p_j, r_j, c_k, \xi_k, a : [0, T] \to [0, \infty)$ be measurable and
bounded, $u \in L^2(0, T)$, initial states $\zeta_j(0) \in \mathbb{R}$ arbitrary, and states $\zeta_j$ absolutely continuous on $[0,T]$ satisfying their equations a.e. (Caratheodory solutions). (Y-form) If $\dot\zeta_j = -p_j(t)\zeta_j + \sqrt{r_j(t)}\,u$ and
$y = \sum_j \sqrt{r_j(t)}\,\zeta_j$, then
$u y = \frac{d}{dt}\tfrac12\sum_j\zeta_j^2 + \sum_j p_j\zeta_j^2$ a.e., so
$\int_0^T u y\,dt \ge -\tfrac12\sum_j\zeta_j(0)^2$. (Z-form) If
$\dot\zeta_k = -\xi_k(t)\zeta_k + \sqrt{c_k(t)\xi_k(t)}\,u$ and
$y = a(t)u + \sum_k\bigl(c_k(t)u - \sqrt{c_k(t)\xi_k(t)}\,\zeta_k\bigr)$, then
$u y - \frac{d}{dt}\tfrac12\sum_k\zeta_k^2 = a u^2 + \sum_k(\sqrt{c_k}\,u - \sqrt{\xi_k}\,\zeta_k)^2 \ge 0$
a.e. For constant coefficients both realise $\sum_j r_j/(s + p_j)$ and
$a + \sum_k c_k s/(s + \xi_k)$.

## Hypotheses

coefficients nonnegative, bounded, measurable in $t$ (no smoothness); finitely many terms.
