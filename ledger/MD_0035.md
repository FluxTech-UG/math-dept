## Statement

For every $g \in \mathbb{C}^N$,

$$ \sum_{j=0}^{N-1} (D g)_j = 0. $$

In particular this holds for $g = \Pi(a, b)$ with $\Pi$ the 3/2-rule dealiased product defined in H3 and H4 of MD_0039, since $\Pi(a, b) \in \mathbb{C}^N$.

## Hypotheses

- **H1.** $N \ge 2$ is even, $L > 0$, $\omega = e^{2\pi \mathrm{i}/N}$; indices $j, m \in \{0, \ldots, N-1\}$.
- **H2 (transform).** $\hat g_m = \sum_{j=0}^{N-1} g_j \, \omega^{-jm}$.
- **H3 (wavenumbers, Nyquist zeroed).** $\kappa_m = 2\pi m / L$ for $0 \le m < N/2$, $\kappa_{N/2} = 0$, and $\kappa_m = 2\pi (m - N)/L$ for $N/2 < m < N$.
- **H4 (derivative).** $(D g)_j = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{m=0}^{N-1} \mathrm{i} \kappa_m \, \hat g_m \, \omega^{jm}$. For real $g$ this is the real-FFT derivative with the Nyquist mode zeroed.
- **H5.** Real data is the case $g \in \mathbb{R}^N \subset \mathbb{C}^N$; the grid is uniform, which is built into H2.

## Why it was raised

Raised by 1DSim4 through C3 of MDR:2026-10-17-rk-linear-invariant: flux-form terms of a pseudospectral right-hand side integrate to zero under the rectangle rule on a periodic grid.

## Current notes

Proved in Lean (MathDeptPrivate/Results/MD_0035.lean): summing over the grid kills every mode except $m = 0$ (orthogonality of the $N$-th roots of unity), and $\kappa_0 = 0$ kills that one. Even $N$, $L > 0$ and the Nyquist convention are unused. The expanded product-rule form $\Pi(a, Db) + \Pi(b, Da)$, which is not of the form $Dg$, is MD_0039.
