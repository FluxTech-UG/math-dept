## Statement

$M$ has an eigenvalue $\lambda$ with $|\lambda - \theta| \le \varepsilon$.

## Hypotheses

- $d \ge 1$, $M \in \mathbb{C}^{d\times d}$ arbitrary (not assumed normal or diagonalizable), $\theta \in \mathbb{C}$, $u \in \mathbb{C}^d$ with $\|u\|_2 = 1$, $\varepsilon \ge 0$ with $\|Mu - \theta u\|_2 \le \varepsilon$ and $\varepsilon < 1 - |\theta|$.

## Why it was raised

Raised by 1DSim4 through C2 of MDR:2026-10-17-nonnormal-residual-certificate.

## Current notes

Refuted, exactly and in Lean. counterexamples/MD_0052_jordan_residual.py: M = [[0, 1], [0, 0]], theta = 1/10, u proportional to (1, 1/10), eps^2 = 1/10100; the only eigenvalue 0 is at distance 1/10 > eps. MathDeptPrivate/Results/MD_0052.lean proves `refuted : not claim` for the literal statement with theta = 5/12, u = (12/13, 5/13), eps = 1/5 (lean-review ACCEPT, claim checked word for word). A residual places theta only in the eps-pseudospectrum (MD_0051); locating an eigenvalue needs conditioning (MD_0053) or a separation argument (MD_0054, MD_0055).
