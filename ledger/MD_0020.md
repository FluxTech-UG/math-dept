## Statement

Assume the common setting H1 and an embedded error estimate E(y, h, theta) in R^d, C^{p+1} in all arguments, with ||E(y, h, theta)|| <= C_E h^{p+1} on K_delta; || . || is the Euclidean norm. Fix phi : (0, inf) -> [phi_min, phi_max], 0 < phi_min < 1 < phi_max, smooth and nondecreasing, with phi(x) <= rho for all x < 1 for some rho < 1 (a rejection shrinks the trial step by a factor bounded away from 1, so every step terminates), a tolerance tau > 0 and an initial trial step h_init(tau) independent of theta. The controller: at t_n with trial step h, set h := min(h, T - t_n) (clip), compute eps = ||E(y_n, h, theta)||; accept iff eps <= tau. If rejected, h := h phi(tau/eps) and retry. If accepted, y_{n+1} = Psi_h(y_n, theta), t_{n+1} = t_n + h, next trial step h phi(tau/eps). Stop when t_n = T. This defines a grid h(theta, tau). The decision pattern is the finite sequence of accept/reject outcomes, clip events, and N. Fix kappa > 0. Let U be an open subset of Theta on which the decision pattern is constant and every eps evaluated at an unclipped trial step satisfies eps >= kappa tau (a clipped final step's eps only decides acceptance and feeds no later step). Claims: (a) theta -> y_N(theta; h(theta, tau)) is C^1 on U. (b) There are tau0 > 0 and C', depending only on the derivative bounds of f and E on K_delta, on T, the tableau, phi, kappa and the C^1 norm of y0, and not on tau or U, such that for tau <= tau0, theta in U and H <= H0 (H0 as in MD_0019 clause (ii)), ||d_theta [y_N(theta; h(theta, tau))] - d_theta y_N(theta; h)|_{h = h(theta, tau)}|| <= C' H^p, H the largest accepted step. (c) Consequently both derivatives differ from S(T; theta) by at most a constant (same dependence) times H^p.

## Hypotheses

- **H1.** Integers d, m, p >= 1. Theta is an open subset of R^m. f : R^d x Theta -> R^d is C^{p+1}; y0 : Theta -> R^d is C^1. An explicit Runge-Kutta method is given by a Butcher tableau (A, b, c) with A strictly lower triangular, of classical order p, with coefficients independent of theta; Psi_h(y, theta) denotes one step of size h. A grid is 0 = t_0 < t_1 < ... < t_N = T with steps h_n = t_{n+1} - t_n and H = max_n h_n. y_N(theta; h) = Psi_{h_{N-1}} o ... o Psi_{h_0} (y0(theta), theta). y(t; theta) is the exact solution of y' = f(y, theta), y(0) = y0(theta), assumed to exist on [0, T]. S(t; theta) solves S' = f_y(y(t; theta), theta) S + f_theta(y(t; theta), theta), S(0) = d_theta y0(theta). Norms on R^d and on d x m matrices are fixed (any choice; constants depend on it). For theta in Theta and delta > 0, K_delta is the closed delta-neighbourhood of the trajectory {y(t; theta) : 0 <= t <= T}.
- **H2.** The embedded error estimate, the controller and the decision pattern as defined in the Statement.

## Current notes

Statement made explicit at triage on 2026-10-17. The request's controller rule had no reject branch, so "acceptance decision" was undefined: the standard accept-iff-eps<=tau rule with retry is stipulated. The request's fixed lower bound eps_0 cannot hold as tau tends to 0 (eps scales with tau), so it is replaced by eps >= kappa tau with kappa fixed and C' uniform in tau. A clipped final step is exempt from that bound, since its eps only decides acceptance. H <= H0 is added because a small tau alone does not force small steps. The bound phi(x) <= rho < 1 for x < 1 was added on 2026-10-17 after S1, before any settlement: without it a rejection need not shrink the step and the controller need not define a grid (MD_0021's witness, which inherits this controller, satisfies it with rho = 1/2). On 2026-10-17 two cross-references in the Statement were resolved without change of meaning: "(S)" is the common setting stated as H1, and "C1(ii)" is MD_0019 clause (ii). S3 (2026-10-17) proved (a), the chain-rule split of the total derivative into the frozen-grid derivative plus the controller term R, the sensitivity of y_N to one step size (f(y_N) + O(H^p)) and the frozen half of (c) (`proofs/MD_0020.md`). Clause (b) is refuted by the exact witness `counterexamples/MD_0020_expanding_step_map.py` (`python -m mdept.refute MD_0020` prints ok, exact): Dahlquist y' = -theta y, explicit Euler, the relative Heun-minus-Euler estimate E = theta^2 h^2/2, phi(x) = (1 + 3v)/(3 + v) with v = (x/2)^6, h_init = tau^(1/2), kappa = 1/4, and for k >= 3 the step h_k = 4/(2k+1) with tau_k = h_k^2 at theta = 1. The accepted-step map sits on a repelling fixed point with multiplier exactly -5, every unclipped step accepts with eps/tau = 1/2, and the final step is a clip; the script certifies by induction that abs(R)/H >= 2^k/90 for every k >= 3, so no tau0, C' and H0 satisfy (b), and the full half of (c) falls with it. An adversarial review by a fresh context on 2026-10-17 accepted the witness: E is admissible because the Statement constrains E only by C^{p+1} smoothness and abs(E) <= C_E h^{p+1}; the quantifier order lets U shrink with tau, since C' may not depend on tau or U. Successor MD_0027 revises this entry with a step-size stability bound sup over x of (p+1) x phi'(x)/phi(x) <= 2 - gamma with gamma > 0, plus a nondegenerate estimate ||E(y, h, theta)|| >= c_E h^{p+1}.
