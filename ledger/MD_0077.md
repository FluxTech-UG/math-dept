## Statement

Let $\Omega_g, \Omega_s \subset \mathbb{R}^d$ be disjoint bounded Lipschitz open sets
with $\Gamma = \partial\Omega_g \cap \partial\Omega_s$ of positive measure, $\Omega = $ the interior
of $\overline{\Omega_g \cup \Omega_s}$, with $\partial\Omega_g \subset \Gamma$ (the gas is enclosed by
solid) and the outer boundary of $\Omega_s$ insulated. Let $C_g, C_s > 0$ (heat capacity per unit
volume) and $k_g, k_s > 0$ be constants. For $s \notin (-\infty, 0]$ let $\theta \in H^1(\Omega)$
solve $s\,C\theta - \nabla\cdot(k\nabla\theta) = s\,C_g\chi_{\Omega_g}$ weakly on $\Omega$ (piecewise
constant $C, k$; continuity of $\theta$ and of $k\partial_n\theta$ across $\Gamma$; zero flux outside),
and put $1 - \tilde f(s) := \langle\theta\rangle_{\Omega_g}$, the mean of $\theta$ over $\Omega_g$. Then
$$1 - \tilde f(s) = w_0 + \sum_{n\ge1} w_n\frac{s}{s + \lambda_n}, \qquad w_0 = \frac{C_g|\Omega_g|}{C_g|\Omega_g| + C_s|\Omega_s|},$$
with $w_n \ge 0$, $\lambda_n > 0$ the positive eigenvalues of $-C^{-1}\nabla\cdot k\nabla$ (zero flux on $\partial\Omega$, which is the outer boundary of $\Omega_s$), and $w_0 + \sum_{n\ge1} w_n = 1$.

## Hypotheses

constants per phase; perfect thermal contact; insulated outer solid boundary.

## Why it was raised

Raised by 1DSim4 through C1 of MDR:2026-10-18-finite-solid-capacity.

## Current notes

Refuted (exact): with $\Omega$ disconnected (a solid component detached from the gas) the offset counts only the solid in the gas's component. `counterexamples/MD_0077_detached_solid.py`: gas $(0,1)$, solid $(-1,0)\cup(1,2)\cup(3,4)$, unit coefficients, $s = 1/100$; $\langle\theta\rangle_g = 0.33444\ldots$ exceeds the bound $26/101$ the Statement forces. MD_0087 carries the statement with $\Omega$ connected. Accepted by an adversarial review on 2026-10-18 (`triage/MD_0077.md`).
