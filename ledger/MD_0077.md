## Statement

Let $\Omega_g, \Omega_s \subset \mathbb{R}^d$ be disjoint bounded Lipschitz open sets
with $\Gamma = \partial\Omega_g \cap \partial\Omega_s$ of positive measure, $\Omega = $ the interior
of $\overline{\Omega_g \cup \Omega_s}$, with $\partial\Omega_g \subset \Gamma$ (the gas is enclosed by
solid) and the outer boundary of $\Omega_s$ insulated. Let $C_g, C_s > 0$ (heat capacity per unit
volume) and $k_g, k_s > 0$ be constants. For $s \notin (-\infty, 0]$ let $\theta \in H^1(\Omega)$
solve $s\,C\theta - \nabla\cdot(k\nabla\theta) = s\,C_g\chi_{\Omega_g}$ weakly on $\Omega$ (piecewise
constant $C, k$; continuity of $\theta$ and of $k\partial_n\theta$ across $\Gamma$; zero flux outside),
and put $1 - \tilde f(s) := \langle\theta\rangle_{\Omega_g}$, the mean of $\theta$ over $\Omega_g$. Then
$$1 - \tilde f(s) = w_0 + \sum_{n\ge1} w_n\frac{s}{s + \lambda_n}, \qquad w_0 = \frac{C_g|\Omega_g|}{C_g|\Omega_g| + C_s|\Omega_s|},$$
with $w_n \ge 0$, $\lambda_n > 0$ the positive eigenvalues of $-C^{-1}\nabla\cdot k\nabla$ (zero flux on $\partial\Omega$, which is the outer boundary of $\Omega_s$), and $w_0 + \sum_{n\ge1} w_n = 1$.

## Hypotheses

constants per phase; perfect thermal contact; insulated outer solid boundary.

## In plain words

A gas region is surrounded by solid, the two exchange heat by conduction through perfect contact, and the outside of the solid is insulated. Heat the gas at a given complex frequency and record its mean temperature. This entry claimed that the response is a constant offset plus a positive sum of relaxation terms, one per decay rate of the combined conduction problem, with the offset equal to the gas's share of the total heat capacity and all the weights adding up to 1. It is false as stated, because the statement lets the solid include pieces that never touch the gas. In one dimension, take gas on an interval of length 1 with solid on both sides of it and a further solid interval set apart, all coefficients equal to 1. The detached solid never receives heat, so at low frequency the gas shares its heat only with the attached solid: its mean temperature approaches 1/3, while the stated offset of 1/4 counts the detached piece and forces a value of at most 26/101 (about 0.257) at the test frequency, where the exact value is about 0.334. The comparison is rigorous, in interval arithmetic.

What survives is the same representation on a connected region, where every piece of solid is in contact with the gas, directly or through other solid, and the stated offset is the right one; MD_0087 carries it. For the consumer, which models a fluid exchanging heat with a solid of finite capacity through such a sum of relaxation terms, this decides that the offset must count only the solid in thermal contact with the gas.

## Current notes

Refuted (exact): with $\Omega$ disconnected (a solid component detached from the gas) the offset counts only the solid in the gas's component. `counterexamples/MD_0077_detached_solid.py`: gas $(0,1)$, solid $(-1,0)\cup(1,2)\cup(3,4)$, unit coefficients, $s = 1/100$; $\langle\theta\rangle_g = 0.33444\ldots$ exceeds the bound $26/101$ the Statement forces. MD_0087 carries the statement with $\Omega$ connected. Accepted by an adversarial review on 2026-10-18 (`triage/MD_0077.md`).
