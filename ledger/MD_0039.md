## Statement

For all $a, b \in \mathbb{C}^N$ (real data included),

$$ \sum_{j=0}^{N-1} \Big( \Pi(a, D b)_j + \Pi(b, D a)_j \Big) = 0. $$

## Hypotheses

- **H1.** $N \ge 2$ even, $M = 3N/2$, $L > 0$, $\omega_N = e^{2\pi\mathrm{i}/N}$, $\omega_M = e^{2\pi\mathrm{i}/M}$; $\hat g_m = \sum_{j=0}^{N-1} g_j \omega_N^{-jm}$, extended $N$-periodically in $m \in \mathbb{Z}$.
- **H2 (derivative).** $D$ is the operator of MD_0035: $(Dg)_j = \frac1N \sum_{m=0}^{N-1} \mathrm{i}\kappa_m \hat g_m \omega_N^{jm}$ with $\kappa_m = 2\pi m/L$ for $0 \le m < N/2$, $\kappa_{N/2} = 0$, $\kappa_m = 2\pi(m-N)/L$ for $N/2 < m < N$.
- **H3 (embedding).** For $g \in \mathbb{C}^N$ and $p \in \{0, \ldots, M-1\}$, $(\mathcal{E}g)_p = \frac1N \Big( \sum_{m=-N/2+1}^{N/2-1} \hat g_m \omega_M^{pm} + \tfrac12 \hat g_{N/2} \big(\omega_M^{pN/2} + \omega_M^{-pN/2}\big) \Big)$: the trigonometric interpolant of $g$, Nyquist mode as a cosine, evaluated on $M$ points.
- **H4 (dealiased product).** $w_p = (\mathcal{E}a)_p (\mathcal{E}b)_p$, $c_q = \frac1M \sum_{p=0}^{M-1} w_p \omega_M^{-pq}$, and $\Pi(a, b)_j = \sum_{q=-N/2+1}^{N/2-1} c_q \, \omega_N^{jq}$ for $j \in \{0, \ldots, N-1\}$: modes below $N/2$ kept, the Nyquist mode of the product zeroed.

## Why it was raised

Raised by 1DSim4 beside C3 of MDR:2026-10-17-rk-linear-invariant: the convective energy flux is discretised in product-rule expansion, $a\,Db + b\,Da$ with each product dealiased, which is not of the form $Dg$ that MD_0035 covers.

## Current notes

Proved in Lean (MathDeptPrivate/Results/MD_0039.lean): $\sum_j \Pi(x, y)_j = N c_0$; on the $M = 3N/2$ grid a product of two mode sums with $|m|, |m'| \le N/2$ has zero mean except on $m' = -m$ (the dealiasing makes this exact); the Fourier coefficients of $Dg$ vanish at $\pm N/2$, so the half-weight Nyquist cosine drops out; the two remaining sums cancel under $m \mapsto -m$ because the signed wavenumber is odd. The zeroed Nyquist wavenumber is load-bearing: kept, the pair sum is nonzero. H3 and H4 match the consumer's real-FFT implementation to round-off on real data (S1 probe and review); on complex data the formulas go beyond what a real FFT computes, and the identification with the code is for real data. $L > 0$ is unused.
