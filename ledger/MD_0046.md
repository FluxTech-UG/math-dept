## Statement

For every $\alpha > 0$ the Hermitian matrix $\alpha K + \operatorname{diag}(h)$ is positive definite. In particular, if $S \subset \{0,\dots,N-1\}$ and the complement of $S$ contains two indices of opposite parity, the principal submatrix $K_{SS}$ is positive definite (apply the first claim with $h$ the indicator of the complement of $S$ and $\alpha = 1$ to vectors supported on $S$).

## Hypotheses

- **(D) Fourier derivative.** $N \ge 2$ is even and $L > 0$. For $n \in \{0, \dots, N-1\}$ put $\bar n = n$ if $n < N/2$ and $\bar n = n - N$ if $n > N/2$, and the symbol $s_n = 2\pi i \bar n / L$ for $n \ne N/2$, $s_{N/2} = 0$. With $\omega = e^{2\pi i/N}$, $D \in \mathbb{C}^{N\times N}$ is $D_{jl} = \frac1N \sum_{n=0}^{N-1} s_n\, \omega^{(j-l)n}$ (indices $j, l \in \{0,\dots,N-1\}$), i.e. $D = F^{-1}\operatorname{diag}(s)F$ with $(Fg)_n = \sum_j g_j \omega^{-jn}$. Then $D$ has real entries and $D^{\top} = -D$ (consequences of the definition, not extra hypotheses), and $K := -D^2 = D^{\top}D$.
- $h \in \mathbb{R}^N$, $h_j \ge 0$ for all $j$, and there are indices $j, k$ with $j - k$ odd and $h_j > 0$, $h_k > 0$.

## In plain words

The Fourier spectral second derivative on an even number of equally spaced points of a periodic interval, built with the highest (Nyquist) frequency set to zero, is a diffusion matrix with two neutral modes: the constant vector and the grid-scale zigzag that alternates +1, -1, +1, -1, which the zeroed Nyquist frequency makes invisible to the derivative. Absorption confined to points of one parity is not enough: the mix of the two neutral modes that is zero on every point of that parity feels no absorption, and the matrix stays singular. The result says that absorption at two points of opposite parity, one at an even index and one at an odd index, kills both neutral modes: diffusion of any positive strength plus that absorption is positive definite. The same argument gives a corollary: if the solution is held at zero on an even and an odd grid point, the diffusion matrix on the remaining points is positive definite.

For the consumer, which discretises diffusion spectrally with absorption on only part of the grid, this decides that every discrete mode decays strictly, with no neutral mode left over, as long as the absorbing points include both parities; and that the reduced diffusion operator on the remaining points is invertible. The result is checked in Lean.
